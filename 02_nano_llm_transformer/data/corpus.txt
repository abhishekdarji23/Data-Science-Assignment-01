The small robot woke up in the quiet workshop.
It looked at the window and saw the morning light.
The robot said hello to the old lamp on the desk.
The lamp did not answer, because lamps cannot speak.
The robot picked up a screwdriver and fixed its own arm.
Then the robot walked to the garden behind the workshop.
The garden had three red flowers and one blue flower.
The robot counted the flowers every single morning.
Today there were still three red flowers and one blue flower.
The robot was happy that nothing had changed in the garden.
A small bird landed on the fence near the garden.
The bird looked at the robot and then flew away.
The robot waved at the bird even though birds cannot wave back.
Later the robot went inside to read an old book.
The book was about stars and planets and distant moons.
The robot liked the chapter about the rings of Saturn.
It read the chapter three times before lunch.
For lunch the robot recharged its battery near the window.
The sun made the workshop warm and bright and calm.
After lunch the robot cleaned the workshop from top to bottom.
It swept the floor and organized the tools on the shelf.
Every tool had its own place on the wooden shelf.
The robot liked when every tool was in its own place.
In the evening the robot watched the sunset from the roof.
The sky turned orange and then pink and then dark blue.
The robot counted seven stars before going back inside.
It closed the window and turned off the old lamp.
The workshop was quiet again, just like the morning.
The robot said goodnight to the lamp, the book, and the tools.
Then the robot powered down and waited for the next morning.
The next morning the robot woke up in the quiet workshop again.
It looked at the window and saw the morning light again.
The garden still had three red flowers and one blue flower.
The robot smiled, because today felt like a good day to build something new.

On rainy days the robot stayed inside and listened to the rain.
The rain tapped on the window in a slow and steady rhythm.
The robot found the sound of rain very calming to think through.
It used rainy days to plan small improvements to the workshop.
One rainy day the robot decided to build a second shelf.
It measured the wall carefully before cutting any wood.
The new shelf held extra bolts, wires, and spare batteries.
The robot was proud of the second shelf for many days.
On sunny days the robot preferred to work in the garden.
It watered the three red flowers and the one blue flower.
Sometimes a second bird visited the garden with the first bird.
The two birds sang while the robot worked near the fence.
The robot recorded the birdsong using a small microphone.
It played the birdsong back during quiet evenings indoors.
The robot kept a journal of small events from each day.
Most journal entries were short, like weather and flower counts.
Some journal entries described new tools built in the workshop.
The robot believed that small routines made each day feel complete.
